上游说明：View factors are calculated based on a face agglomeration array (finalAgglom generated by faceAgglomerate utility). Each view factor between the agglomerated faces i and j (Fij) is calculated using a double integral of the sub-areas composing the agglomaration. The patches involved in the view factor calculation are taken from the qr volScalarField (radiative flux) when is greyDiffusiveRadiationViewFactor otherwise they are not included.
